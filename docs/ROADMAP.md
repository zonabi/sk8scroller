# Roadmap

Each milestone ends with something you can hand to a friend. Task cards are in BACKLOG.md.

| | Milestone | Outcome | Cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ | **M0 Prototype (v0.1)** | Core loop playable in a browser: run, jump, grind, balance, switch-ups, air tricks, combos, bails, XP, 4 skaters. Placeholder art. | — |
| ◐ | **M1 Feel** | It's fun for 10 minutes on a phone. Done: sound, run recording, tuning panel, forgiving balance and inputs (P-01…P-04, P-07), push (P-05), fakie (P-06), turn mid-jump (P-12), touch button pads that mirror the keyboard (P-13, P-14), harder rails with no mashing or hop-reset exploits (P-15…P-18), drop off a rail (P-17), progression pacing (P-11), how-to-play card, playable tutorial, pause and settings. First phone sessions played 2026-11-20/21. Left: his verdict on the trial items (P-07, P-11, P-13, P-14, P-18, tutorial); mid-trick slams and ignored trick presses still uninvestigated; manuals and landing quality on hold at his request. | T-01, T-03, T-06, P-09, P-10 |
| ◐ | **M2 Look** | 16-bit art generated in code, graffiti UI, loading screen, 3D spin turnaround, fakie sprite set — all approved on Mac. Added: depth on stacked decks, fence and shopfront looks. Left: animation depth, hand-drawn sheets if wanted. | T-07, T-08, T-10b |
| ▢ | **M3 On the phone** | Capacitor iOS build on TestFlight with haptics, tilt verified, safe areas. On hold at his request until the web build settles. Still unreported from the phone: `?fps` readout, audio start. | T-11 … T-13 |
| ◐ | **M4 Depth** | Special meter, loadouts, tagging, stairs/gaps/multi-level terrain, 3 districts. Started early: a second course, STACKS, with rails above rails (T-16a–d). The districts idea has become a draft plan for cities and stages: `CITIES.md` (5 cities × 3 stages, plan and concept art only). | T-14 … T-18 |
| ▢ | **M5 Retention** | Daily seed + leaderboard, missions, cosmetics, music. | T-19 … T-22 |
| ▢ | **M6 Launch** | Store page, trailer, analytics, crash reporting, Android. | later |
